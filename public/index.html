THE PROBLEM

Campus problems should not
disappear into silence.

📡

Scattered Reporting

Campus concerns may be reported through disconnected channels, making it difficult to track them in one place.

👁️

Limited Visibility

People who report an issue may not receive clear updates about its progress.

⏱️

Slow Communication

Students, teachers, and authorities need a clearer way to coordinate around campus concerns.

❓

Unclear Accountability

Without a visible resolution process, it can be difficult to know whether an issue has actually been addressed.

💬

Missing Feedback

Universities need a structured way to collect feedback and understand whether reported problems have been resolved satisfactorily.

THE UNIVOICE APPROACH

One platform. A clearer path from
complaint to resolution.

01

Report

Students and teachers submit complaints with relevant details.

02

Prioritize

Community voting helps highlight issues that affect more people.

03

Resolve

Authorized personnel review complaints, document corrective action, and mark issues as fixed.

04

Verify

The person who submitted the complaint confirms whether the issue has been resolved and can provide feedback.
